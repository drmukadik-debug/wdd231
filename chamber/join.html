NP Membership

NP Membership is designed for nonprofit organizations.

  • No membership fee
  • Access to chamber networking activities
  • Community networking opportunities
  • Basic chamber resources

Bronze Membership

Bronze Membership provides basic business benefits.

  • Business networking opportunities
  • Access to selected training activities
  • Business directory listing
  • Member communication opportunities

Silver Membership

Silver Membership provides additional visibility and benefits.

  • All Bronze benefits
  • Additional training opportunities
  • Event discounts
  • Additional business promotion

Gold Membership

Gold Membership provides premium chamber benefits.

  • All Silver benefits
  • Priority business promotion
  • Home page spotlight opportunities
  • Premium networking opportunities
  • Special event opportunities
