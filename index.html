About Me

My name is Alain Mukadi Kabeya, and I am from the Democratic Republic of the Congo. I am interested in technology, web development, healthcare, and community development. I enjoy learning new technologies and developing practical skills in HTML, CSS, and JavaScript. My background in healthcare has inspired me to explore how technology can improve access to information and services. I also enjoy serving my community and supporting projects that promote health, education, and development. My goal is to combine my healthcare experience with web development skills to create useful digital solutions that can make a positive difference.

I am currently developing my skills in HTML, CSS, JavaScript, and modern web development through the WDD 231 course. This course is helping me strengthen my understanding of responsive design, web accessibility, and interactive web development while gaining practical experience building real-world websites.
